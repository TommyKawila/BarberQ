# Market positioning

## What it is

A LINE-native queue booking platform for barbershops. One shop = one tenant. The shop keeps its LINE OA; customers are not asked to install a separate consumer booking app.

This describes **currently implemented / verified product behavior**, not commercially proven outcomes.

## What it is not

- Not an all-in-one salon OS (POS, inventory, CRM, payroll, marketing automation).
- Not a generic AI receptionist or reminder/revenue product.
- Not a marketplace that replaces the shop’s own brand.

Feature-count leadership is not the strategy.

## Who is who

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| **Buyer / economic decision-maker** | Shop owner |
| **Operational users** | Owner and barbers/staff |
| **End user** | Customer booking the shop |

Do not describe barbers as paying buyers.

## Primary ICP

Independent shops of approximately **2–8 barbers**.

### Strong qualification signals

- customers book ahead
- customers choose barber
- LINE / LINE OA is important
- owner/admin repeatedly answers booking questions
- barber schedules differ
- owner operates mainly from mobile
- enough appointment volume to create coordination workload

### Lower-priority early segments

- one-person shops
- almost entirely walk-in shops
- extremely price-sensitive shops
- enterprise chains
- complex multi-branch salons

## Core emotional value (positioning, not evidence)

ไม่ต้องเปิด LINE เช็กคิวให้ลูกค้าทีละคนอีก

**Positioning principle:** sell outcomes before features.

## Pricing

**HYP-004:** 599 THB / month / shop is a **hypothesis**, not validated pricing truth.

## Differentiation (intended, not proven superiority)

- barber-specific workflow
- barber-first experience
- multi-barber clarity
- LINE-first
- mobile-first
- fast setup
- done-for-you onboarding
- reduction of booking-chat workload

## Claims

Do not invent claims about lost revenue, missed bookings, no-shows, or quantified business results.
